# Submission requirements (H1)

Read from the live NASA Space Apps guides on 2026-11-14 (by the build agent; a person should still skim both pages once):
- Project Submission Guide: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/
- Judging & Awards Guide: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/

## Deadline
- Submission closes **Sunday 15 November 2026, 11:59 p.m., local time of the team's Local Event** (Universal Event: Team Owner's local time). Late saves or submissions are not judged.
- Projects can be edited and resubmitted until then; only the last saved submission is judged.
- To submit: tick "Terms and Conditions" and "Confirm All Work is Original", then click "Submit for Judging". A banner confirms success.

## Demo: one of these, not both
- **Video, 30 seconds or less**, or **slide deck, up to 7 slides including title and intro slides.** Exceeding either limit makes the project ineligible for Global Judging.
- Hosted outside Space Apps (YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub, …) with a public link: no login, permission or registration. Test it in a private window.
- English required. A video may be spoken in another language only with English subtitles. Slides must be in English.
- Slides must show: team name, names of team members, the challenge selected; an image of the project; the user experience; how NASA / space-agency data make it possible.
- Suggested video structure: 0:00 names and team, 0:02 challenge and motivation, 0:05 project title and tagline, 0:10 how it works and the NASA data used, 0:20–0:30 what it could do.
- The demo counts toward the Presentation score, but most of the evaluation is based on the project page.

## SI (AI) rules
- SI use is allowed, but not to come up with the idea for solving the challenge.
- Generated content may not use or modify NASA branding (logos, flags, mission identifiers). Checked 2026-11-14: the app uses its own CSS logo and no NASA images.
- Must be marked where it is used: **code and data: "descriptive text and metadata"**; images and videos: a visible watermark; audio: descriptive text and metadata. Judges see these markers and judge "originality, intent, and execution" of the SI-assisted parts.
  - In the app: summaries and findings carry `generated_by` in the data and an on-page note. The README and project page should also say the code was written with Claude Code.
  - Any AI-generated image or video in the demo needs a watermark.

## Eligibility
- All members registered, on the team's Members tab, and confirmed (not waitlisted) for the same Local Event; 1–6 members (Universal Event: at least 2). Members cannot be added after submission closes. (H2, still a person's check.)
- No images, video, audio or likeness of anyone under 18.
- Teams are reviewed under NASA's China funding restriction.

## Recommended, not required
- For a new website, request a FortiGuard Web Filter Classification so judges' networks do not block it (Category: e.g. "Education" or "Web-based Application"; Company Name: "N/A").

## Judging
Scored 1–5 on each of: Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance (including NASA data integration), Presentation (demo plus full project). Global Finalists are announced 17 December 2026.
